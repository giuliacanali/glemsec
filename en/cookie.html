1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that visited sites send to the user's device, where they are stored and later sent back to the same sites on the next visit.

2. Cookies used by this site

The site does not use profiling or marketing cookies. It uses technical cookies necessary for the site to work, statistical analytics cookies (enabled only after consent via the banner), and, indirectly, cookies set by third-party services embedded in the pages.

Service Purpose Type
Google Analytics (GA4) Anonymous, aggregated statistics about site visits Analytics cookie, enabled only after consent via the banner
Google Maps Displaying the map with the business location Third-party cookies/scripts
Google Fonts Loading the site's typefaces Request to third-party servers
Google Places API Retrieving the average Google review score Server-to-server call, no cookie set on the browser
Web3Forms Sending the contact form by email Server-to-server request when the form is submitted
WhatsApp (wa.me) Opening the WhatsApp chat when the relevant buttons are clicked External redirect, cookies managed by WhatsApp/Meta

3. How to manage cookies

You can manage or disable cookies directly from your browser settings. Disabling technical cookies may affect the correct functioning of some parts of the site (e.g. the map).
