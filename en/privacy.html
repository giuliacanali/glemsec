Processing is based on the explicit consent provided by the user when submitting the form or requesting the discount code (art. 6.1.a GDPR) and, for the subsequent phase, on the performance of pre-contractual measures requested by the data subject (art. 6.1.b GDPR). The site also uses Google Maps to display the map and Google Places API to show the average review score: these services are provided by Google LLC under its privacy policy.

6. Data retention

Data collected through the contact form is kept for as long as necessary to handle the request and any resulting business relationship, and in any case no longer than the legal terms for commercial and tax communications. The email address provided for the discount code is kept until consent is withdrawn; where marketing consent has been given, the email is kept until unsubscription or withdrawal of consent.
